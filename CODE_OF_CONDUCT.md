# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Pulse a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating
empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback,
taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting
comments, harassment, public or private intimidation, publishing another person's private
information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, fair, and
protect the privacy and safety of people who report an incident.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project
in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior directly to the maintainers
using private contact information on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a
public issue for a sensitive conduct report. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly,
and their privacy and security will be respected.

Consequences may range from a private correction or warning to a temporary or permanent ban,
depending on impact, intent, and repeated behavior.
